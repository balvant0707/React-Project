# Home and new requests

**Home** is your start page. It greets you by your first name with a short line that changes every day, and shows what is on your plate. It leads to creating new tasks with one tap.

## Home {#home}

From top to bottom:

- **Birthdays:** a line with the colleagues who have a birthday today; on your own birthday the banner wishes you a happy birthday.
- **Quick access:** tiles for **My tasks**, **New request**, **To decide** (only when something waits for you, with the number), **Messages**, **Find colleagues** (opens the people page with the search field ready), **Leave** (without leave rights: **Help**) and, where you have the rights, **Planning board**, **Assets**, **Items** and **Tickets**. They always stand in one row, the most important first: a phone shows 4 to 6 of them, a wide screen as many as fit.
- **My tasks:** up to four short lists, each with its number: **Overdue** (your tasks whose due date has passed), **Today** (your tasks due today), **Coming up** (your other open tasks) and **In the team** (open tasks of your teams that nobody has taken, with the button **I take it**). A list without tasks is left out. On a phone they are tabs, on a computer columns. Each list shows two tasks (four on a computer); scroll inside it for more, it stops at the next task. **Show all** opens the full filtered list.
- **At a glance:** short links with a number, only for what you may use and only when the number is above zero; a tap opens the page behind it (no lists here). Everybody: **My trainings due** and, with leave rights, **Away today** (as faces: colleagues of your teams first, then the others, six at most and "+N") and **My leave** (days this year: taken, approved and still ahead, requested, with a small bar; the app has no balance). Depending on your rights: **Trainings due in the team**, **Overdue tasks** and **Waiting for a prerequisite** (team leads), **Team days over the limit** (supervisors above, HR), **Tasks waiting for material** and **Below minimum stock** (items, stock), **Job descriptions to review** (HR), **Unassigned tickets** (service desk) and **Locked accounts** (user management).
- **News:** pinned posts and the posts of the last two weeks from the groups you follow, as cards you swipe sideways, with the first picture of the post and the picture of the group when there are any. A tap opens the post.

## Tasks and numbers {#dashboard}

Above the task list the big numbers and lists of the former start page have moved.

The page shows big numbers. Click a number to open the matching filtered list. The (i) icon next to a number explains what is counted. Hover over (or tap) a small trend line to see the value of a week.

- **Open in my team:** open tasks of your teams that nobody has taken yet. A tap opens the list, where every task has **I take it**.
- **Due in 7 days (mine)**, **Open tasks**, **Overdue tasks**, **Done in 7 days:** counters for the tasks you may see.
- **Waiting for a prerequisite:** open tasks that wait for a prerequisite subtask, for example a purchase. They still count as open and, if their due date has passed, as overdue.
- **Open tickets:** only for people who may see tickets. Without that right the number is not shown.
- **Leave:** **Leave to decide** (only when requests wait for you), **Away today** (people of your teams and below you), **Team days over the absence limit** (only for the supervisors above and HR, when there are any) and **My leave** (approved working days this year). They open the leave page.

Under **Open tasks**, **Overdue tasks**, **Done in 7 days** and **Open tickets** a small line shows the trend of the last 12 weeks (one point per week, the last one is today; hover over the line to see the value of a week). Next to the number the change is written with an arrow: green when the number moved the good way, red the bad way. The line itself is dark blue, green for **Done** and red for overdue tasks. **Overdue** counts by the original due date, so postponing a task does not make the delay disappear from the trend; the number itself uses the current due date and can differ.

In the lists every task starts with the icon of its task type, with status and due date on the right ("tomorrow", "3 days overdue"). The bars in front of the number show the priority: the more, the more urgent.


## To decide {#decisions}

Under **To decide** you find everything that waits for your decision: leave requests of your team, purchase requests, and any other approval an administrator builds into a workflow. Each card shows what matters for the decision; the buttons (for example *Approve*, *Reject*) decide with one click. A decision that needs a comment asks for it; one that needs further fields opens the task. The page has three tabs: **To decide** (open decisions, they disappear once taken), **My requests** (your own tasks of types that contain approvals, with who they are waiting for and since when; an administrator switches this per task type) and **Decided by me** (the decisions you took, with your comment). A big number on [the task list](help:my-work#dashboard) shows how many are waiting.

## New request {#start}

Under **New request** you start anything that runs a route: an order, an application, a repair, an idea. You see cards for the task types you may start, each with its type's icon and colour, for example "Purchase request" or "General task". There is also a card for every ticket queue you may report in (subtitle "Report a ticket", for example "IT Service Desk"); it opens the ticket form for that queue.

1. Type a keyword at the top ("order gloves", "repair") or pick a card.
2. Describe your request or the task in the form.
3. Click **Start**. The task now runs its route, and everyone involved is notified.

Team, person and due date come first, because they decide the task; asset and worksheet follow under "More details", unless the type hides them. For running tasks see [Tasks](help:tasks).

> If nothing fits, create the task under **Tasks → New task**. An administrator decides which types appear on the start page.
