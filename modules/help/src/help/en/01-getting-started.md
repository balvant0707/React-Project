# Getting started

COMPASS is the working tool for tasks, assets, incidents and people in the company. This chapter shows how to sign in and find your way around.

## Signing in {#sign-in}

You sign in with your **e-mail address** and password. If no e-mail address is stored for you, use your **username** instead; it is usually `firstname.lastname` in lower case with umlauts written out (`jürgen.müller` becomes `juergen.mueller`), and your [profile](help:profile) shows it. COMPASS then asks for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (second factor), which is checked as soon as you have typed the sixth digit. The first time you set the app up: scan the QR code, type the code, done.

If your account uses Microsoft sign-in, click the Microsoft button. There is no password then, and Microsoft counts as the second factor too.

- **Forgot your password?** Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your e-mail address (or your username). If an e-mail address is stored for you, a link arrives (valid for one hour). Without one, HR gives you a new **activation sheet** in person. Accounts with Microsoft sign-in have no password.
- **New account?** HR or administration gives you the login: either by e-mail with a link to set the password, or with an **activation sheet** (QR code, username, code). Scan the QR code, choose a password and set up the authenticator app. The code is valid for seven days and only once.
- **On a phone:** add COMPASS to the home screen first, then sign in inside the app. The app has its own sign-in, separate from the browser.
- **Access locked?** Then your access was locked; contact HR or your supervisor.
- **Sign out** is at the top right (on a phone as the last entry under **More**).

## The screen {#layout}

The **menu** is on the left. You only see entries you have rights for. If you have many, the menu gathers them in groups: **Goals & tasks**, **Assets & items**, **People** and **Settings**. A click on a group folds it in or out; the browser remembers which are open. With only a few entries you see them without groups. Drag the right edge of the menu to make it wider or narrower (double click = default width); the arrow at the bottom folds it down to the icons. The header at the top has:

- the **search** (see below),
- the **question mark** for help on the current page,
- the **bell** with your notifications,
- the **DE / EN** switch for the language and the switch for **dark mode**,
- your photo and name: a click opens your [profile](help:profile).

On a phone (narrow windows) a bar sits at the bottom edge, as in Teams or Instagram: **Home**, **Tasks**, **Messages**, **More** and on the far right **Search**. **More** opens the whole menu with **New request** and **To decide** at the top. Numbers on the entries show what is unread or waits for you. At the top there are the logo and on the right the help, the bell (opens your notifications) and your photo (name, **My profile**, language, light/dark, sign out); the bar scrolls away with the page. On a task or ticket its buttons take the place of the bar. Every page starts with a dark blue head with its title and, where there is one, its main action as a wide button (such as **New task**). Lists appear as cards; more than two quick filters sit behind the **Quick filters** button, and the **Sort** button replaces clicking the column headers. In the task list both sit, together with the other filters, behind the **Filter** button next to the search box; a red dot shows that a filter is set. The main buttons of a task or ticket stick to the bottom edge.

## Search {#search}

The search box at the top (on a phone the **Search** tab at the bottom, with the field at the bottom edge, right above the keyboard while typing) searches everything you may see: tasks, tickets, assets, people, positions, trainings, goals, comments and posts in groups of the last 12 months, and document names. Hits from this manual show up too, in the group "Help".

- **Shortcut:** `Ctrl+K` (Mac: `⌘K`) or `/` jumps to the search box.
- It starts at **2 characters**. In titles and names small typos are forgiven ("kompresor" finds "Kompressor"); comments, post texts and descriptions are searched exactly as you type.
- **Posts** are found by their title, text or a comment in them; a click opens the post under [Messages](help:messages#conversation).
- **Numbers:** "#14" or "14" finds the task or ticket with number 14.
- Arrow keys select, `Enter` opens, `Shift+Enter` opens in a new tab.

> Search needs a connection. Only the help hits also work offline.

## Help in COMPASS {#help}

There are two ways to get help:

- **The question mark in the header** opens a panel on the right with the chapter that fits the current page. You can search there or pick another topic. "Open in manual" jumps to the full view.
- **The manual** (menu entry "Help") has all chapters with its own search and a table of contents.

## Key terms {#terms}

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Asset | A machine, plant or place in the asset tree, with a QR code. |
| Ticket | A report: incident, request or inspection finding. Anyone can file one, even anonymously through the QR code. |
| Task | Work that runs through a defined route and is done by a team or a person. |
| Initiative | A large task with subtasks and progress. |
| Series | A recurring task, for example a monthly inspection. |
| Worksheet | Points to tick off inside a task. |
| Team | A group of people. Teams are the rows of the planning board. |
| Role | A bundle of rights that an administrator assigns to you. |
| Step | A station in the route of a task, for example "Approval". |

## Working offline {#offline}

Without a network COMPASS shows the data you loaded last, and a red bar tells you so. Worksheet entries and photos stay on the device and are sent once you have reception again. Everything else needs a connection. On the [asset page](help:assets#offline) you can make documents available offline.
