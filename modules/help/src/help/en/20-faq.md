# Frequently asked questions

## I cannot see a menu entry or an asset {#cannot-see}

You only see what your roles grant rights for. For assets, rights on a node cover everything below it. If something is missing, ask an administrator.

## Why are the action buttons on a task greyed out? {#greyed}

The current step is meant for another role or person. The line "Waiting for: …" names who is up. See [Routes and approvals](help:tasks#routes).

## I can only read and comment on a ticket {#ticket-read-only}

Then you have the right to "check", not to "edit". Answer in the feed, the team sees it. See [Tickets](help:tickets#ticket-page).

## I moved a date and had to write a reason {#reason}

That is intended. The original due date is kept for the statistics, the reason is in the feed. See [Punctuality](help:statistics#punctuality).

## I have no network {#no-network}

Saved data stays readable. Worksheet entries, photos and QR reports are sent automatically later. Everything else needs a connection. See [Working offline](help:getting-started#offline).

## I cannot reset my password {#password}

If your account uses Microsoft sign-in there is no password. Sign in with Microsoft. Otherwise use "Forgot password" with your e-mail address or username: the link comes only if an e-mail address is stored for you. Without one, HR gives you a new activation sheet.

## Why can an asset not be deleted? {#delete-asset}

It still has child assets, tickets or tasks. Remove or move those first.

## How do I find a particular task again? {#find-task}

Use the [search](help:getting-started#search) with title, keyword or number ("#14"). Comments of the last 12 months and document names are searched too.
