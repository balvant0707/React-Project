# My profile and notifications

## Profile {#profile}

Under **My profile** (click your name at the top; on a phone your photo at the top right, then **My profile**) you see your details, including the **username** you sign in with. Name, position and photo are maintained by HR or an administrator. You set here yourself:

- **Language:** German or English, applies to your account on all devices.
- **Change password:** 10–72 characters. Accounts with Microsoft sign-in have no password.
- **Show my birthday to others** (under **Privacy**): day and month appear in the directory and on the start page. Switched off, your birthday disappears there; only HR still sees it.

## The bell {#bell}

The bell (top right; on a phone **Activity** in the bottom bar) collects notifications: mentions, comments on your tasks, new assignments, steps waiting for you, reminders and training deadlines. A click opens the item. Everything that belongs to a conversation (mentions, comments, posts, direct messages) is not in the bell but under [Messages](help:messages), with a number of its own. Reactions to your messages stay in the bell. New messages appear briefly as a pop-up while COMPASS is open. Read messages are deleted after 30 days.

## Push notifications {#push}

With **push**, important notifications appear on your phone or computer even when COMPASS is closed: mentions (also of the whole team), direct messages, comments, new posts and comments in groups you follow, assignments, tasks waiting for you and forwards. Reminders and summary lines are not pushed, they stay in the bell and the e-mail. Tapping the notification opens the item. It arrives about 90 seconds after the event, and only if you have not read it by then.

To switch push on: **My profile** > **Push notifications** > switch **On this device**, then allow the browser's question. It works per device: switch phone and computer on separately. The list below shows every device that gets push; remove an old one there with the bin.

- **iPhone and iPad:** push works only in the app on the home screen (iOS 16.4 or newer). In Safari tap **Share** > **Add to Home Screen**, open COMPASS from there, sign in and switch it on.
- **Android, Windows, Mac:** Chrome, Edge or Firefox; the installed app works the same.
- **Blocked?** If you declined the browser's question, allow notifications in the site settings (lock icon next to the address) and reload the page.
- While COMPASS is open and in front you see the pop-up in the app instead of the notification.
- **Signing out** switches push off on this device, so nobody on a shared device gets your notifications.
- **Quiet hours:** with the switch **No push from … to …** no device buzzes during that time (e.g. 22:00 to 06:00, also across midnight). What arrives then is in the bell and under Messages but is not pushed afterwards.

## Choosing notifications {#message-switches}

Under **Notifications** you switch off the lines you do not want: comments on your tasks and tickets, new posts in groups, replies in posts, direct messages and reactions. **Mentions always arrive.** A conversation you muted stays silent anyway. Changes apply immediately.

## Setting reminders {#reminders}

Under **Reminders** you choose what you are reminded of:

- tasks planned for today,
- tasks that are due soon (and how many days ahead),
- overdue tasks (every 3 days until done),
- trainings that are due soon, expired or missing,
- items that have to be ordered now because of their lead time.

Reminders arrive on weekdays early in the morning. If a task belongs only to a team, COMPASS reminds all members as long as nobody has taken it. Changes apply immediately.

## E-mail {#email}

If e-mail delivery is switched on, COMPASS sends **one summary per day** in the afternoon, only if something unread is waiting. With the switch "Also by email" you turn it off for yourself.
