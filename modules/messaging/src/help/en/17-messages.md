# Messages

Everything written in COMPASS in one place: comments on the tasks, tickets, assets and goals you are involved in, and the posts of your groups. It is not a chat in the sense of "answer at once": everything stays findable on the record or in the group.

## Page layout {#layout}

How the page looks depends on the width of the screen:

- **Wide screen:** three columns. On the left **For me**, **My groups** and **Discover groups**, in the middle the conversations of your choice, on the right the selected conversation. A selected group shows its posts wide over the middle and the right.
- **Narrower screen:** a tree on the left. The newest conversations hang below "For me" and below every group; **Show more** loads more. The arrow folds a group in or out, the ⋯ menu next to it offers "New post", the bell and "Mark all read". The selected conversation is on the right.
- **Phone:** tiles at the top (swipe sideways) switch between "For me" and your groups, groups with something new come first; **New team** at the end; below them the conversations of the chosen tile, two lines each (title and time, then group, sender and text), with **Unread only** and **Mark all read** in the ⋯ menu next to the tabs; the round **+** button at the bottom right starts a new message; a conversation opens full screen like in a messenger (the field to write in stays above the keyboard), the arrow at the top left goes back.

So you no longer click away: the conversation appears next to the list. **Open** at the top right jumps to the task or ticket itself.

## For me {#for-me}

It lists the conversations you **take part in**, newest activity first. You take part automatically when you created a task or ticket, it is assigned to you (also through a step of its route), you wrote something or somebody mentioned you with **@Name** or your team with **@Team**. New posts of groups you follow with "Every new post" show up here too.

A line shows what it is about, the last message and a red number for what is unread; **@ Mentions you** marks a mention. What you show is read. Filters: **Work items** or **Posts**, **Unread only**; **Mark all read** clears the numbers and the message notifications in the bell. The number on the menu entry counts the conversations under "For me" with something unread.

> Looking is not taking part. Opening a task does not put it into your list.

## Read and write in a conversation {#conversation}

A conversation reads like a chat: the oldest messages at the top, the newest at the bottom right above the input box, your own on the right, the others on the left with their names. It opens at the bottom; scroll up and it loads older messages. On tasks and tickets, status changes and other events appear as grey lines in between. On a phone, the chat button on a task, ticket, asset or goal opens exactly this conversation here under **Messages**, even before anybody has written; the arrow at the top left leads back to the record, and so does a tap on its title. On a computer the history sits on the page, newest first with the input box above it.

Write at the bottom, in one line that grows with the text: text, emoji (the smiley button in the field offers a few common ones; any other emoji you type or paste with the system keyboard works as well), **@Name**, pictures, files and linked tasks through **+**; the round button on the right sends, on a computer `Ctrl+Enter` too. The arrow on a message replies to it (the reply shows the original as a quote); ⋯ edits your own messages (for 15 minutes after writing them). Only people with the right *Delete messages* delete.

## Mentions in posts {#post-mentions}

In a post you mention people the same way as in a message: type **@** and pick a name from the list (the arrow keys and Enter work, the whole team too where you may mention it). The mention is shown **red and bold** in the post, and the person is notified. Emoji, linked tasks (cards) and files work in the post input as in the message input; both are one input with the same buttons.

## Task cards {#task-cards}

**Link a task** (button under the input box) puts a task into your message as a card: type, number, title, priority, who works on it, due date, category and a short description, with the buttons **Open task** and **Discuss in chat** (the conversation of the task). A card shows only what the reader may see; someone without access to the task sees "Task not visible to you". Cards also work in the comments of a record. The menu ⋯ of a message offers **Create task from message**: the form starts with the first line of the text, and the new task appears as a card under the message.

## Reactions {#reactions}

The smiley on a message reacts with 👍, ✅, ❤️, 😂, 😮 or 🙏; on a phone a long press on the message does the same. Each reaction then shows below the message with its count. On a computer, hover over it to see who reacted, and click to set or take back your own. On a phone, a tap on the reactions opens a list with all names; a tap on a line there sets or takes back your own. Everybody who may read the message can react, on tasks, tickets and posts in groups too. A ✅ often replaces a "Done". Reactions show up for everybody at once. Whoever wrote the message gets one line per message in the bell ("Anna reacted 👍"), unless the conversation is muted or set to "mentions only". The line counts as read as soon as you read the conversation.

## Polls {#polls}

While writing a post, **Add a poll** attaches a poll; the text of the post is the question. Enter two to ten different options and choose whether **several options** may be chosen and whether the poll is **secret** or **public**. Polls exist only in posts of groups, and everybody who may post in the group can add one.

Everybody who may read the post can vote: choose an option and press **Vote**. While the poll runs you can change or take back your vote. A poll has no end date: it runs until whoever created it closes it with **End now**. Everybody sees how many people have voted, but the **result only once it is ended**; whoever created the poll sees it at once. An ended poll cannot be opened again.

- **Secret:** nobody sees in COMPASS who voted for what, not even the person who created the poll. In small groups the running result may still give away who just voted.
- **Public:** with the result, each option shows who chose it (click the number of votes).

The bars show the share of the people who voted; in a multiple-choice poll they add up to more than 100 %. Deleting the post deletes its votes too.

Options, multiple choice and secret/public cannot be changed after posting; the text of the post can be corrected for 15 minutes as usual.

## Seen by {#seen}

On your newest message (in the chat and in the history of tasks and tickets) an eye next to the time shows how many people have seen it. Click it to see their names. The number updates when the conversation loads and with every new message. Seen means: the person had the conversation open afterwards. It works both ways: whoever may read the conversation also sees that you have read it.

## New conversation {#new}

A new conversation is a **post in a group**: open the group first (under **My groups**; follow it via **Discover groups** if it is not there yet) and press **New post** in the group header or in the group's ⋯ menu. Write a text (bold, lists and links work), add pictures or a PDF and press **Post**; a title is optional. The first picture is shown across the top of the post (with "+2" when there are more); a tap shows all of them full screen. The conversation of a task or ticket starts with the first comment there.

Whether you may post depends on the group: either **everybody who reads the group** starts posts, or **only the owners** (for example in the group "Unternehmen" for announcements to everybody). Everybody who may read may comment.

For **15 minutes** after posting you can still correct your post or comment (⋯ > Edit); after that it stays as it is. Deleting is the exception: only people with the right *Delete messages* do it (Manager and HR by default), for their own texts too; a group owner without that right does not delete. A deleted message also leaves the others' bell (entirely while unread, without its text once read); e-mails and push messages already sent cannot be called back. Nobody changes other people's texts.

## Mention the whole team {#team-mention}

With **@** and the team name (marked "whole team" in the list) you reach **every member** of the team, also those who muted the group. For each member it counts as a personal mention: bell, "Mentions you" under "For me", and from then on they take part in the conversation.

You can only mention the team of the conversation itself: in a post its group, on a task or ticket the team of the record. On records everybody who may comment may do it. In posts the owners decide whether only they or every member may mention the team. Use it for what everybody really must know.

## Direct messages {#direct}

Press **New message** at the top of the page (on a phone the round **+** button at the bottom right; **Write a message** on a person's page). **To whom?** searches your chats, teams and people; without a search term it shows your latest chats and your teams. A chat opens with one tap, a team with the field for a new post in its channel. People are collected at the top under **To:**; **Open chat** opens the chat of exactly them, an existing one or a new one. You write in the window that opens. There is one conversation per pair of people; it appears in **For me** (filter **Chats**) as soon as somebody wrote, with the other person's picture. Only the two of you can read it, nobody else, not even administrators or managers.

You can correct a message for 15 minutes, and you can **delete your own messages** (⋯ > Delete); nobody deletes someone else's. The bell of the other person shows one line per conversation. **Mute** or **hide from my Inbox** with the bell above the conversation if you do not want to be notified; a new message from the other person brings it back.

> **Nothing confidential or private belongs here.** COMPASS keeps messages like all other data. Use e-mail, phone or a talk for such topics. Writing to someone who left or whose access is blocked is not possible any more; you can still read the old conversation.

## Group chats {#group-chats}

If you pick several people under **To:**, you get a **group chat**: for quick coordination among a few people without making a team. Only the people in it read and write. With ⋯ in the chat header you give it a **name** (otherwise it shows the names of the people), **add people** (they see the whole history so far) or **leave the chat**; after that you read nothing in it until somebody adds you again. As in direct messages everybody deletes only their own messages, and nothing confidential belongs in it. For a group that works together for good (with tasks and posts), make a [team](help:messages#new-team).

## Create your own team {#new-team}

For a group of people, make a **team** instead of a group chat: press **New team** at the top of the page (on a phone at the end of the strip with your groups), give it a name and a short description. You become its **owner** and first member. In the **team settings** (⋯ menu of the team) you add the other members, rename the team and decide who may post and who may read. Tasks can be assigned to the team, and it has its own posts and chat.

A team made by you does not change who may see personal data of others (trainings, suitability reviews): that depends on the supervisor, not on teams. HR keeps the settings that tie a team to training requirements, positions and leave planning.

## Follow and mute {#follow}

Above every conversation there is a bell button. **Follow** brings the conversation under "For me" even when you are not involved otherwise. Once you follow, choose **All messages**, **Only @mentions** (then only what mentions you counts) or **Mute** (stays in the list, counts nothing). **Stop following** takes it out of your list.

## Groups {#groups}

Every team is a **group** and can carry posts. **My groups** lists the groups you are a member of, keep or follow; the number shows new posts. Under **Posts** a group shows its posts as a feed, pinned ones on top, then the newest: picture, text, poll, reactions and the number of comments; The newest comment stands under each post, so you see at once where the conversation is. **Comment** or a click on the comment opens the post with its replies; on a computer it then stands on the right beside the posts, the arrow closes it again. **Everything** adds the conversations on the tasks, tickets and goals of the team that you may read, mixed by newest activity.

Who may read a group depends on it: only its **members**, or **everybody**. A group everybody reads is listed under **Discover groups**; **Follow** puts it under "My groups". That does not make you a member; an admin sets the members.

For a group's notifications, set its bell (in the group header or the ⋯ menu) to **Every new post**, **Only @mentions and replies** or **Muted**. "Every new post" is the default for your teams and for groups where only the owners post.

## Owners and moderation {#moderate}

The **owners** of a group keep its posts. They always post, pin posts and decide in the group's **Settings** the **picture** of the group (shown in the list, the header and on the start page), **who posts**, **who reads** and **who may mention the whole team**. An admin or whoever manages teams chooses the owners; an owner need not be a member. An owner also maintains the **members** of the group in its settings, **renames** the group and can **hand the ownership over** to another member; an owner may add themselves. Members get the team rights of their roles on the team's work; every change is logged. Whoever holds the right to moderate (Manager and HR by default) may do this in the groups they belong to and in groups open to everybody, not in the groups of others. A deleted post disappears for everybody, with its comments.

> Posts are not for confidential matters: whoever may read a group sees every post in it.
