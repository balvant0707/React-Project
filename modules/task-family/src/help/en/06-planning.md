# Planning board

The planning board distributes tasks over days and people. Rows are a team and its members, columns are days.

## Layout {#layout}

- **Choose a team:** The first row belongs to the team itself and shows tasks assigned to the team but not yet to anybody. A row per member follows.
- **Timeline:** Mon–Fri (weekend optional), starts one week back and shows 12 weeks. It grows as you scroll. The arrows page by week, **This week** jumps back.
- **Backlog** on the right: tasks without a planned date and without a deadline in the visible range. You can fold it in and out.

## Planning tasks {#plan}

Drag a card onto a day and a row. This sets the planned date and the person. From the backlog you drag it there; back to the backlog removes the date. The order inside a cell is set by where you drop.

- A task with a due date but no planned date appears dashed with "Due" on its due day. If you move it, it becomes planned, the due date stays.
- If you drag a card into a person's row, the task is assigned to that person afterwards.
- Every move appears in the task's feed. Changes by others show up live.

A filter shows only the tasks of one [initiative](help:initiatives#planning).

> There is one day per task. There is no capacity planning.

## On a phone {#phone}

The grid does not fit a small screen, so the board shows **one day at a time**. Use the arrows or the date field at the top to change the day, **Today** jumps back. Below, the team and each member are sub-headings with their cards of that day. The backlog is a folded section at the bottom.

Cards are not dragged on a phone (that would fight with scrolling). Tap the move button on a card, choose the team or person and the date, and save. Clearing the date puts the task into the backlog.
