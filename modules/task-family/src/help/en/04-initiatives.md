# Initiatives

An initiative is a large task with many subtasks, such as a project. It shows the progress of all tasks below it.

## The list {#list}

The search box at the top left narrows the initiatives by title; the head and table header stay in place while you scroll.

On a phone the initiatives use the same cards as the task list: a coloured edge marks initiatives with overdue tasks, and more cards load as you scroll.

Under **Initiatives** every initiative appears with a progress bar and the number of overdue tasks. A click opens it. **New initiative** creates one. Any task without a parent can become an initiative later: choose *Make this an initiative* in its menu. Which route it follows depends on the task type you pick. Turning it off again only works while no goal and no buckets are attached. An initiative can belong to a [goal](help:goals).

## The initiative page {#page}

Four tabs:

- **Activity:** the feed with comments, like any task.
- **Tasks:** all subtasks in three views (see below).
- **Worksheet:** the initiative's own points.
- **Evaluation:** punctuality and progress of the tasks below.

Every task that has subtasks gets the same tabs.

## Tree, board, Gantt {#views}

In the section with the subtasks you choose the view:

- **Tree:** subtasks as a table with progress. The arrow folds levels, "+" adds subtasks.
- **Board:** columns by **category**, **status** or **person**. Drag cards from column to column. You create, rename and sort categories yourself. "Add suggested categories" creates Planning, Execution and Acceptance.
- **Gantt:** bars from planned date to due date. Choose the range (1, 3, 6 or 12 months). Drag a bar to move it, or its edges to change planned date and due date. For the due date COMPASS asks for a reason as usual. Tasks without a date are not shown.

On a phone the board shows one column at a time (choose it with the buttons on top; **⋯** on a card moves it to another column), and the Gantt chart is read-only with a narrow label column.

## On the planning board {#planning}

From the board a link leads to the [planning board](help:planning), filtered to this initiative. The task list can also be filtered by initiative.
