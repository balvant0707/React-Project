# Goals

Goals show where the company wants to go and which work contributes. They form a tree of goals and sub-goals.

## Goals and sub-goals {#tree}

Under **Goals** you create a goal with **New goal** and a subordinate one with **New sub-goal**. Every goal has a responsible person and a target date. Show achieved and dropped goals with "Show achieved and dropped". A goal with sub-goals can only be deleted once they are gone. The **Org chart** shows goals graphically.

## Measure and check-ins {#measure}

A goal can have a **measure**: a number that moves, such as hours, percent or euros. You define what is measured, the unit, the start value and the target value.

- With **Add check-in** you enter the current value. If the last check-in is too old, the goal shows "Check-in overdue".
- Without a measure a goal only gives direction. Its progress is then derived from the sub-goals ("derived").
- The **status** is: On track, At risk, Off track, Achieved or Dropped.

> A piece of work ("launch the website") is an [initiative](help:initiatives), not a measure.

## Assigning initiatives {#initiatives}

You attach initiatives to a goal. The goal shows the assigned initiatives. Conversely every task shows which goal it contributes to ("This task contributes to"), if you have the right.
